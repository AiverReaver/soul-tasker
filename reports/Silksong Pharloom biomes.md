# Pharloom splits into thirty-odd painted biomes

Hollow Knight: Silksong has no canonical area count. Depending on what you count, the answer is **18, 23, about 30 or about 45**. Fextralife lists **18 main locations**, and its full list reaches **about 45** once secondary areas, hubs, chapels and weavenests are added ([Fextralife](https://hollowknightsilksong.wiki.fextralife.com/Locations)). PCGamesN traces **23 areas** along the critical route ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hollow-knight-silksong/map)). For theming, the useful number is **about 30 visually distinct biomes**, 3 of which (The Abyss, Verdania, Red Memory) are gated behind Act 3. Each region has its own palette. One reviewer contrasts vibrant, green Moss Grotto with nearly colorless Bone Bottom and reads it as Pharloom's fading beauty ([Backloggd](https://backloggd.com/u/ExtravaGav/reviews/)). Sourced visual detail is thin: press and wiki give at most one phrase per area. Most descriptions below are therefore inferred from the area name and general knowledge of the game, and are marked as such. Six areas cover the color wheel and both light and dark modes: Moss Grotto, Choral Chambers, Deep Docks, Mount Fay, Bilewater and The Abyss.

## Counts diverge because sources nest sub-areas differently

PCGamesN counts only the critical path and splits the Underworks into West and East. It places **Blasted Steps at the end of Act 1**, runs **Act 2 from the Underworks to The Cradle**, and opens **Act 3 with The Abyss** ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hollow-knight-silksong/map)). Fextralife adds 13 secondary locations, 3 hubs (Bone Bottom, Bellhart, Songclave), 5 chapels and 5 weavenests ([Fextralife](https://hollowknightsilksong.wiki.fextralife.com/Locations)). The community wiki instead nests districts under parent regions. For example, it calls Choral Chambers the main and largest area of the Citadel ([hollowknight.wiki](https://www.hollowknight.wiki/w/Choral_Chambers)). In Act 3 the void spreads across **nearly the whole map** and re-tints earlier areas ([Backloggd](https://backloggd.com/u/ExtravaGav/reviews/)). Some act placements are unconfirmed, including Hunter's March, Sands of Karak, Memorium and Bilewater.

## Six areas span the wheel and both modes

The strongest UI theme set pairs one dominant hue with one accent per theme. **Moss Grotto** gives green on dark ([PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hollow-knight-silksong/map)). **Choral Chambers** gives gold and ivory, a light regal theme with a crimson accent ([hollowknight.wiki](https://www.hollowknight.wiki/w/Choral_Chambers)). **Deep Docks** gives orange on iron black, a warm dark theme. **Mount Fay** gives ice blue and white, a cool light theme. The Mount Fay pick rests on the area's name only; no source describes it. **Bilewater** gives a murky bile green-yellow ([hollowknight.wiki](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Bilewater)). **The Abyss** gives void black and violet, the darkest option. **Greymoor**'s rain-grey slate works as a calm neutral default ([hollowknight.wiki](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Halfway_Home)). Alternates are Shellwood (aqua and pink), Sands of Karak (sand and coral) and Far Fields. Far Fields sits too close to Choral Chambers' gold to earn its own slot.

## Conclusion

The game names do more for theming than the sourced facts do. Only about a dozen areas have any published visual description, and **no source gives color values for any area**. Treat every palette above as a starting hypothesis. Before shipping, sample real colors from official screenshots or the [promo-art category](https://www.hollowknight.wiki/w/Category:X_SS_Promo_Art). Then check each theme against WCAG AA contrast. Several picks, such as green, bile green and orange, separate cleanly for color-blind users only if their luminance also differs.
