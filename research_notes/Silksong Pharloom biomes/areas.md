# Silksong Pharloom areas/biomes: names, count, visuals

Source caveat: hollowknight.wiki, Fandom, Nintendo Life, Pocket Tactics, JetPunk and Filibuster all returned 403/402 to direct fetch. Wiki content below comes from search-result snippets of hollowknight.wiki pages, which is less reliable than reading the full page. PCGamesN and Fextralife were fetched in full.

## How many distinct named areas/biomes are in Silksong (main game; Act 3 separately)? Do sources count differently?

### Takeaway
No single canonical count. Sources range from 18 "main" areas (Fextralife) to 23 (PCGamesN route) to about 45 named locations (Fextralife's full list, which adds hubs, chapels, weavenests and sub-areas). A practical biome list for theming is about 30 distinct regions. About 3 of them are Act 3-gated (The Abyss, Verdania and Red Memory), and Act 3 also re-colors much of the existing map.

### Cited Findings
- PCGamesN lists 23 areas in route order: Moss Grotto, The Marrow, Deep Docks, Far Fields, Greymoor, Bellhart, Shellwood, Blasted Steps, Underworks West, Choral Chambers, Whiteward, Underworks East, Mount Fay, Whispering Vaults, High Halls, Cogwork Core, The Cradle, Bilewater, Weavenest Atla, The Abyss, Verdania, Hunter's March, Sands of Karak — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hollow-knight-silksong/map)
- PCGamesN on acts: Blasted Steps is the final location of Act 1. Act 2 runs from the Underworks to The Cradle. The Abyss is the first area reached in Act 3 — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hollow-knight-silksong/map)
- Fextralife groups its list as follows — [Fextralife Locations](https://hollowknightsilksong.wiki.fextralife.com/Locations):
  - 18 Main Locations: Moss Grotto, The Marrow, Deep Docks, Far Fields, Greymoor, Shellwood, Bellhart, Blasted Steps, Grand Gate, Choral Chambers, Underworks, Cogwork Core, Whispering Vaults, Mount Fay, High Halls, The Cradle, Red Memory, The Abyss.
  - 13 Secondary Locations: Hunter's March, Wormways, Sinner's Road, The Mist, Exhaust Organ, The Slab, Memorium, Whiteward, Sands of Karak, Wisp Thicket, Bilewater, Verdania, Putrified Ducts.
  - 3 Hubs: Bone Bottom, Bellhart, Songclave.
  - 5 Chapels: Architect, Beast, Reaper, Wanderer, Witch.
  - 5 Weavenests: Absolom, Atla, Cindril, Karn, Murglin.
  - Grid-only extras: Bonegrave, Mosshome.
- A fan quiz puts Wormways and Sinner's Road in Act 1, and Wisp Thicket, The Slab and Putrified Ducts in Act 2. Unverified fan source — [JetPunk via search snippet](https://www.jetpunk.com/user-quizzes/2543113/silksong-areas-subareas)
- Sub-area relationships from wiki snippets — [hollowknight.wiki Choral Chambers](https://www.hollowknight.wiki/w/Choral_Chambers); [hollowknight.wiki Putrified Ducts](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Putrified_Ducts); search snippet:
  - Choral Chambers is the main and largest area of the Citadel.
  - Putrified Ducts connects to Memorium and Bilewater, and has the Pale Lake sub-area.
  - Sinner's Road leads through The Mist and the Exhaust Organ.
- In Act 3, a reviewer reports that the void affects nearly the whole map — [Backloggd review, ExtravaGav](https://backloggd.com/u/ExtravaGav/reviews/)

### Inferences
- Why counts differ:
  - PCGamesN counts only the critical-path areas, with Underworks split into West and East.
  - Fextralife counts hubs, chapels, weavenests and sub-areas (The Mist, Exhaust Organ, Pale Lake) as separate locations.
  - The wiki nests sub-areas under parent regions. For example, the Citadel contains Choral Chambers, Whiteward, Underworks, High Halls and other districts.
- Suggested working list of distinct biomes (about 30), by journey position:
  - Early (Act 1): Moss Grotto, Mosshome, Bone Bottom, The Marrow, Deep Docks, Wormways, Far Fields, Hunter's March, Greymoor, Sinner's Road (with The Mist and Exhaust Organ), Bellhart, Shellwood, Blasted Steps.
  - Mid (Act 2): Grand Gate, Choral Chambers, Songclave, Underworks, Whiteward, Cogwork Core, High Halls, Memorium, Whispering Vaults, Mount Fay, The Slab, Wisp Thicket, Bilewater, Putrified Ducts, Sands of Karak, The Cradle.
  - Act 3: The Abyss, Verdania, Red Memory. Plus a void-tinted overlay on earlier areas.

### Gaps
- Act placement of Hunter's March, Sands of Karak, Memorium and Bilewater could not be confirmed. Fetches of the wiki and Nintendo Life were blocked, and PCGamesN's order contradicts itself (Bilewater appears before Act 3).
- Fextralife places Verdania and Red Memory in Act 3 only through my inference. The PCGamesN text supports Verdania as late-game.

## Per-area visuals (palette, materials, lighting, mood)

### Takeaway
Sourced visual detail is thin: press and wiki give one phrase per area at most. The hex values below are approximations from general knowledge of the game's art. No source verified them, so check each against screenshots before shipping UI themes.

### Cited Findings
- Moss Grotto is encased in green moss. The Marrow has bone-like surfaces and gets hotter toward lava. Deep Docks has flowing lava and metal chains. Bellhart is filled with bells. Blasted Steps is barren, with sandstorms and strong winds. Verdania lies in ruins, and a reconstructed version appears in a quest — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hollow-knight-silksong/map)
- Choral Chambers: grand, gold-adorned halls draped in silk, with golden bells. It was once a holy place of song and is now in disrepair. The Citadel was built as "both a church and a cage" — [hollowknight.wiki Choral Chambers](https://www.hollowknight.wiki/w/Choral_Chambers); [The Citadel](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/The_Citadel) (search snippets)
- Greymoor is a wasted, rain-soaked region of caverns and fields — [hollowknight.wiki snippet](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Halfway_Home)
- Shellwood is a lush subterranean forest on the pilgrims' route — [hollowknight.wiki Shellwood](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Shellwood) (snippet)
- Bilewater has swampy, noxious tunnels overgrown with roots and thorns. Citadel waste fouled its waters, but the far east below Pale Lake stays clean — [hollowknight.wiki Bilewater](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Bilewater) (snippet)
- A reviewer contrasts green, vibrant Moss Grotto with mostly colorless Bone Bottom. They read this as Pharloom's fading beauty, with each area using its own palette — [Backloggd, ExtravaGav](https://backloggd.com/u/ExtravaGav/reviews/)
- Polygon (quoted) calls the art direction more colorful and vivid than Hollow Knight's — [Fnac L'Éclaireur roundup](https://leclaireur.fnac.com/article/620395-hollow-knight-silksong-que-vaut-vraiment-le-jeu-lavis-des-critiques/)

## Which 4–6 areas have the most distinctive, contrasting palettes (UI theme candidates)?

### Takeaway
Six candidates cover the color wheel and both light and dark modes:
- Moss Grotto: green, dark.
- Choral Chambers: gold and ivory, light and regal.
- Deep Docks: orange on black, dark and warm.
- Mount Fay: white and ice blue, light and cool.
- Bilewater: sickly green-yellow, dark and murky.
- The Abyss: void black and violet, darkest.

Greymoor (slate blue-grey, calm) works as a neutral default.

### Cited Findings
- Moss Grotto is green and moss-encased, and a reviewer calls it vibrant — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hollow-knight-silksong/map); [Backloggd](https://backloggd.com/u/ExtravaGav/reviews/)
- Choral Chambers has gold-adorned, silk-draped halls — [hollowknight.wiki](https://www.hollowknight.wiki/w/Choral_Chambers)
- Deep Docks has lava and metal chains — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/hollow-knight-silksong/map)
- Bilewater is a noxious, root-choked swamp — [hollowknight.wiki](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Bilewater)
- Greymoor is rain-soaked — [hollowknight.wiki snippet](https://hollowknight.wiki/w/Halfway_Home)
- The void spreads across the map in Act 3 — [Backloggd](https://backloggd.com/u/ExtravaGav/reviews/)

### Inferences
- Mount Fay as the cool light theme rests on its snowy-peak name and general knowledge. No source here describes it visually.
- Each pick pairs one dominant hue with one accent:
  - green with lime
  - gold with crimson
  - orange with iron
  - ice with slate
  - bile with brown
  - black with violet

  The six stay distinguishable for users with color-vision deficiency only if luminance differs too. Check contrast (WCAG AA) per theme.
- Alternates:
  - Far Fields: warm gold, but too close to Choral Chambers.
  - Shellwood: aqua and pink, a lighter green option.
  - Sands of Karak: sand and coral.

### Gaps
- No source ranks areas by visual distinctiveness. This selection is my judgment from the palette table.
