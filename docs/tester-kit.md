## 2. How to copy your tester report

The form asks you to paste a short report. It holds counts only (how often you struck, fell, came back), never your goals, tasks or notes.

1. Open Hearthrise.
2. Tap the **Settings** icon on the home screen.
3. Tap **Copy tester report**. "Copied." appears under it.
4. Paste it into the form's report box.

## 3. Feedback form (same form at day 7 and at the end)

Plain words, one question per screen if the form tool allows. All optional except the report box.

1. Which check-in is this? (Day 7 / End of test)
2. **Paste your tester report here.**
3. Holding to strike a task: satisfying, fine, or annoying? Why?
4. When you fell (declared a failed attempt or missed a day), how did the screen after it make you feel?
5. Did the lost embers left behind make you want to come back, or avoid opening the app?
6. After a bad day, did you come back the next day? What helped or got in the way?
7. Does the attempt counter feel like pride or like a record of failure?
8. The evening reminder: helpful or nagging?
9. Deadlines on tasks: motivating or stressful?
10. Losing health when you miss a promise: a lesson or a punishment?
11. **Has Hearthrise ever left you feeling worse?** (Never / Sometimes / Often)
12. If it ever did: when, and what was happening?
13. Anything else? One thing you'd change?

## 4. Running the test (checklist)

- [ ] Day 0: invite at least 16 people (12 must stay opted in for 14 consecutive days; verify the current rule in Play Console Help).
- [ ] Daily, days 0 to 14: check the opted-in count in Play Console. If it drops toward 12, invite more.
- [ ] Day 7: send the form reminder.
- [ ] Day 28: send the final form reminder.
- [ ] Copy each response into the results sheet: one row per tester per form.
- [ ] Crash fixes ship as a new upload (build number + 1), test first. Tone changes wait for the gate review.
