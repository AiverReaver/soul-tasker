Five palettes of 22 colour tokens plus 4 glass tokens (core/palette.dart). One accent (silk) per biome; crimson-class accents mark only what matters. Choose a biome to repaint every specimen on this page.

Space and shape

A 4 pt grid (Space.xs 4 · sm 8 · md 16 · lg 24 · xl 40 · xxl 56), 20 px screen sides, 48 px minimum touch targets. Content uses the needle-cut frame: a 1 px edge round a clipped fill, one radius in three sizes. State changes the edge colour, never the shape.

Liquid glass

Glass is chrome only: the tab bar, circle buttons, pills, switches, hold buttons, sheets and alerts. Layers bottom to top (ui/glass_surface.dart): drop shadow (black 35%, blur 14, 8 px down), backdrop blur σ16 (alert 24, sheet 28) with saturation 1.7, fill (white 8%), a 2 px key line in glassKey, a 5 px bottom shade at 28%, a 2 px inner ring at white 16%, a sheen on the top third at 152°, a 4 px conic rim light, and a glint arc with a dot at the top left. Presses scale to 1.04 over 350 ms with easeOutBack, the only overshoot in the app.

Icons and art

All drawn in code on a 24-unit grid with square caps and round joins, stroke 1.6 (ui/stroke_icon.dart, ui/nav_icon.dart). The boss is an original ink figure in a 200 × 180 box (ui/boss_art.dart), redrawn in three jittered frames every 140 ms so the line boils. Relic emblems gain nicks with attempts.

Motion

Slow, never bouncy; exits faster than entrances. The only overshoot is the glass press. Reduce motion (system or in Settings) stills all of it.
