# Hearthrise Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [date]_

Hearthrise is a task tracker that runs entirely on your phone.

## What we collect

Nothing. Hearthrise has no account and no sign-in, and it makes no network requests. The app does not include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries.

## What stays on your phone

Your bosses, tasks, attempts, lessons, embers, relics and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your phone by the app itself. No server is involved.

## Tester reports

During closed testing, the Settings screen has a "Copy tester report" option. It copies counts (for example, how many tasks you struck) to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it somewhere yourself. The report never includes your boss names, task titles, lessons or inscriptions.

## Google Play

Google Play may collect data about installs and crashes under Google's own privacy policy. We receive only the aggregated crash and performance reports Play Console shows to developers.

## Children

Hearthrise is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.
